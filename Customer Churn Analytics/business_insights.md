## Business Insight 1

### Observation
Month-to-month contracts have the highest number of customers.

### Business Insight
Customers appear to prefer flexible contracts over long-term commitments. This may indicate that flexibility is an important factor in customer acquisition, but it could also increase the risk of customer churn.

### Recommendation
Offer loyalty rewards, bundled services, or discounted annual plans to encourage customers to switch to longer-term contracts and improve retention.
