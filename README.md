# 📊 Customer Churn & Retention Analytics Pipeline

> An end-to-end Data Analytics project that analyzes customer churn in a telecom company using **Python, PostgreSQL, SQL, and Power BI**. The project focuses on identifying churn patterns, customer behavior, and business insights through an interactive executive dashboard.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Customer churn is one of the biggest challenges faced by subscription-based businesses. Losing existing customers directly impacts revenue and customer lifetime value.

This project demonstrates a complete analytics workflow, starting from raw customer data to business insights, using industry-standard tools and practices.

The objective is to:

- Identify customer churn trends.
- Understand factors contributing to churn.
- Analyze customer behavior.
- Create an interactive dashboard for business stakeholders.
- Provide actionable recommendations for improving customer retention.

# 💡 Key Business Insights

- Overall customer churn rate is approximately **26.54%**.
- Month-to-Month contracts experience the highest customer churn.
- Fiber Optic customers contribute significantly to churn.
- Bank Withdrawal is the most commonly used payment method among churned customers.
- Long-term contracts demonstrate better customer retention.
